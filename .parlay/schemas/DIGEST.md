## Schemas with no diagnostics of their own

- `design-spec.schema.md` — Generated by `/parlay-reference-design-spec`.
- `errors.schema.md` — Closed vocabulary for the `errors:` list on a capability operation declared in `spec/intents/<feature>/capabilities.yaml`.
- `feature-structure.schema.md` — Parlay projects use three zones with strict ownership:
- `layout.schema.md` — A **layout** describes the visual / structural composition of a page using components drawn from the active adapter's `componentVocabulary:` (see `adapter.schema.md` Section 8).
- `operation-kinds.schema.md` — Closed vocabulary for the `kind:` field on a capability operation declared in `spec/intents/<feature>/capabilities.yaml`.
- `policies.schema.md` — Closed vocabulary for the `policies:` list on a capability operation declared in `spec/intents/<feature>/capabilities.yaml`.
- `schema-versioning.schema.md` — This is a cross-cutting policy doc, not a file schema — it does not describe an on-disk artifact of its own.
- `steps.schema.md` — Closed vocabulary for the `steps:` list on a capability operation declared in `spec/intents/<feature>/capabilities.yaml`.
